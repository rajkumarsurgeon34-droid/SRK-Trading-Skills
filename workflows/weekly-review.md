# Workflow: Weekly Review

**When:** Saturday. **Time:** 20 minutes.

1. **india-trade-journal** `review --from <Monday>` — net P&L, win rate, expectancy, charges share
2. Find the best and worst setup and the losing hours
3. Write **one** rule change (e.g. "no trades 9:15–9:30", "max 4 trades") and apply it to **intraday-risk-guard** with `--rules`
4. Monthly: compare with last month; if drawdown > 10%, paper-trade a week
